Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Our standard check-in time is 12:00 PM and check-out time is 10:00 AM. We can accommodate early check-ins based on room availability.

Do you assist with Jungle Safari bookings?

Yes, we assist our guests in booking Elephant and Jeep Safaris inside Jaldapara National Park. Please mention this requirement while booking as slots fill up quickly.

Is the property safe for families and couples?

Absolutely. We provide a highly secure environment with 24/7 staff support, free private parking, and a peaceful eco-friendly atmosphere perfect for families and couples.
