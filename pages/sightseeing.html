Nearby Sightseeing

Explore the magnificent wildlife, nature spots, and scenic beauty surrounding Jaldapara National Park.

The Gateway to Dooars

Must-Do Wildlife Safaris

Your trip to Rhino Inn is incomplete without experiencing the raw, untouched beauty of the deep forest. We assist all our guests with booking these iconic excursions.

Iconic Experience

Jaldapara Elephant Safari

The absolute best way to explore the deep core forest trails of Jaldapara is on elephant back. This early morning adventure offers unparalleled, extremely close-up views of the majestic Indian one-horned rhinoceros, wild elephants, and vibrant peacocks hiding in the tall grass. A truly memorable, once-in-a-lifetime adventure for nature lovers.

Thrilling Adventure

Deep Forest Jeep Safari

Feel the adrenaline rush as you navigate through the dense, sprawling canopy of Jaldapara in an open-top Gypsy. The Jeep Safari covers vast stretches of the national park, increasing your chances of spotting herds of deer, bison, wild boars, and the elusive leopards that call this rich biodiversity hub their home.

Explore More

Beautiful Regional Attractions

Buxa Jayanti

A stunning blend of history and breathtaking valley views. Trek through the dense forests to explore the historic ruins of Buxa Fort and the pristine Jayanti riverbed.

Cooch Behar Palace

Explore the historic royal town of Cooch Behar, famous for its magnificent, European-style Rajbari palace and incredibly rich cultural heritage.

Sikia Jhora

A peaceful and relatively undiscovered nature spot near Jaldapara National Park, highly famous for stream boating through dense canopy and rare bird watching.

South Khairbari Eco Park

Located near the Dooars gateway, South Khairbari is a popular leopard rescue center and rehabilitation area offering battery-operated car tours.

Phuentsholing (Bhutan)

Cross the border to explore this vibrant gateway town leading straight into the magical Himalayan kingdom of Bhutan, featuring beautiful monasteries and architecture.

Chilapata Forest

Discover the mysterious and beautiful Chilapata dense forest. This elephant corridor offers an adventurous wildlife trail and the ruins of the Nalraja Garh.
