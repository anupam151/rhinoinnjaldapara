Overview & Description

Our Double A.C Room is designed for a highly comfortable and relaxing stay. Whether you are returning from an adventurous early morning elephant ride or a hot jeep safari through the dense forest trails, this climate-controlled sanctuary ensures complete relaxation, deep sleep, and rejuvenation. Enjoy the natural beauty outside, knowing you have complete comfort inside.
