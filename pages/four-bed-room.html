Overview & Description

Our Four Bed Room is meticulously designed for a highly comfortable and relaxing group stay. It is fully equipped with 2 large Queen-size beds, giving you the flexible option to book it with or without Air Conditioning (A.C.). It features a fast-heating geyser, high-speed Wi-Fi, and a pristine attached bathroom with hot and cold water. It is absolutely ideal for families and small groups looking for convenience and premium comfort in the heart of the Dooars.

With generous interior floor space and multiple bedding setups, it allows families and friend groups to stay together comfortably rather than splitting up into separate rooms. Enjoy the serene greenery right outside your window while planning safaris and experiencing the incredible wildlife of Jaldapara together.
