Overview & Description

Our Deluxe Room A.C (Luxury Four Bed configuration available) is beautifully designed for a highly comfortable, upscale, and deeply relaxing stay. It is fully equipped with 1 premium King-size bed, a powerful Split Air Conditioning unit, a fast-heating geyser, and complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi. The suite also features an immaculately clean, upgraded attached bathroom with 24/7 hot and cold water access.

Ideal for discerning guests looking for ultimate convenience and premium comfort, this flagship suite offers refined interiors, a deeply peaceful ambiance, and excellent proximity to the wildlife sanctuary. It serves as a highly sophisticated retreat in the Dooars where you can unwind in absolute luxury after an adventurous day of Elephant and Jeep safaris.
