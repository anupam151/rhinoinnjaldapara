Overview & Description

Our Standard Double Room is meticulously designed for a comfortable, authentic, and deeply relaxing stay. The room is fully equipped with 1 large King-size bed, a 24/7 hot and cold water geyser, and high-speed Wi-Fi, along with a pristine attached bathroom. It is absolutely ideal for guests looking for convenience, premium comfort, and value right in the heart of the Dooars.

Surrounded by tranquil nature and rich biodiversity, this room provides the perfect basecamp for wildlife enthusiasts, couples, and solo travelers wanting to explore Jaldapara National Park. Wake up to the soothing sounds of birds and the fresh forest breeze right outside your window, preparing you perfectly for a day of adventure.
