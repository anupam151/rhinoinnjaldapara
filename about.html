ABOUT US

Discover the story, heart, and soul behind Rhino Inn Jaldapara.

Welcome to

Jaldapara's Premier Eco Haven

Rhino Inn Jaldapara is a serene eco-friendly homestay located near the famous Jaldapara National Park, right at the gateway popularly known as Jaldapara “0” Point. Surrounded by lush green forests, open grasslands, and the rich biodiversity of the Dooars region, our homestay offers a peaceful retreat for travelers who wish to experience the beauty of nature in its purest form.

Designed to blend naturally with the surrounding environment, Rhino Inn Jaldapara provides comfortable and thoughtfully designed cottages that reflect the charm of the forest landscape. Our property features spacious open areas, greenery all around, and a calm atmosphere that allows guests to truly disconnect from the noise of city life.

Our Philosophy & Vision

At Rhino Inn, we believe that hospitality is about creating memorable experiences. Our team is dedicated to providing warm, friendly service so that every guest feels at home while enjoying the unique charm of the Dooars. Whether you are relaxing within the property, enjoying local cuisine, or simply spending time in nature, we strive to make your stay comfortable and refreshing.

Our location makes Rhino Inn Jaldapara an ideal base for exploring the incredible wildlife and natural beauty of the region. Guests can easily plan jungle safaris inside Jaldapara National Park, known for its population of the Indian one-horned rhinoceros, as well as elephants, deer, and numerous bird species. Nature walks, village visits, and cultural experiences in nearby communities offer a deeper connection with the local lifestyle and traditions.

Perfect for nature lovers, wildlife enthusiasts, families, couples, and peaceful travelers, Rhino Inn Jaldapara offers a calm and memorable stay experience close to the wilderness. Whether you are visiting for a wildlife adventure or simply seeking a quiet escape in nature, our homestay welcomes you to relax, explore, and create unforgettable memories in the heart of the Dooars.

Our Connection to Jaldapara & Local Culture

Operating right at the gateway of Jaldapara gives us a unique responsibility toward our local community. We source our daily ingredients locally, supporting regional farmers and vegetable growers to ensure that every meal served at Rhino Inn is farm-fresh, organic, and bursting with authentic Bengali flavors.

We also work closely with certified local naturalists and forest guides who possess generational knowledge of the Dooars wildlife corridors. When you book a safari or a village tour through us, you are directly contributing to the sustainable livelihoods of the indigenous families who call this magical forest edge their home.
